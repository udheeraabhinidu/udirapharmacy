About UDIRA PHARMACY

Your trusted healthcare partner, serving Sri Lanka with excellence and compassion

Our Story

UDIRA PHARMACY was founded with a simple yet powerful vision — to make quality healthcare accessible to every Sri Lankan family. Under the dedicated leadership of Saman Karunarathna, our pharmacy has grown into one of the most trusted healthcare providers in the region.

With our Chief Pharmacist Dinusha Madhushani leading our pharmaceutical team, we ensure that every customer receives expert guidance and the highest quality medicines. Our commitment to excellence has earned us the trust of thousands of families across Sri Lanka.

As we look to the future, our Administrator Udheera Abhinidu is driving digital transformation to bring our pharmacy services into the modern era, making healthcare more convenient and accessible than ever before.
